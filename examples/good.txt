Three apps loading on every page is the usual reason a Shopify store drops from 2.8% to 1.6% on mobile checkout.

Last month I fixed the same thing for a skincare store: mobile load went from 7.1s to 2.3s and checkout conversion recovered to 3.1% in two weeks. Before and after report: https://example.com/skincare-speed

For your store I would:
1. Audit the theme and the three new apps, and measure what each one adds to mobile load.
2. Defer or replace the heavy app scripts, and clean up the Liquid that renders above the fold.
3. Re-test checkout on real phones and send you the before and after numbers.
